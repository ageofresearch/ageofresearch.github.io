# Code of conduct

Participants must engage with mathematical and technical claims precisely and
respectfully. Criticism should identify evidence, definitions, statements, or
proof steps rather than attack contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, and deliberate misrepresentation
are not accepted. Private or sensitive material must not be posted without
authorization.

Report sensitive conduct concerns through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ageofresearch/formagization/security/advisories/new)
and begin the subject with `Conduct report`; do not include unnecessary
personal data. Technical and scientific disagreements should normally remain
in public issues so the evidentiary record is preserved.
