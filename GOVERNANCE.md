# Governance

## Maintainer role

Archive maintainers curate metadata, enforce reproducibility requirements, and
preserve review records. They do not establish mathematical correctness or
novelty by administrative decision.

## Decisions

- New artifacts and material corrections are reviewed through pull requests.
- Statement-changing corrections require a new artifact version.
- Published tags and release assets are immutable.
- A withdrawn or disputed artifact remains listed with its reason and evidence.

## Conflicts of interest

Authors, maintainers, and reviewers disclose financial, professional, and
collaborative conflicts relevant to an artifact. Author review is recorded as
author review, never independent review.

## AI systems

AI systems are disclosed as tools and may not be listed as accountable authors
or reviewers. Human contributors remain responsible for submissions and review
claims.

## Appeals

Disagreements should be raised in a public issue linked to the artifact record.
The archive preserves the original record, the challenge, responses, and any
resulting correction.
