The scientific boundary

“The kernel accepted it” is one fact—not the whole verdict.

A proof assistant can establish that a formal proposition follows from stated definitions and foundations. That does not automatically establish that the proposition matches the original conjecture, that a domain expert endorses the definitions, that another person reproduced the build, or that the result is novel.

formAGIzation records those questions independently, so strong evidence remains visible without turning missing evidence into an implied claim.

Current formalization

One artifact, three exact conclusions.

View archive
01

Exact equality · k = 2

Two partition statistics agree pointwise.

FD(j, 2, n) = FO(j, 2, n)

For every natural j and n, including the documented boundary convention at n = 0.

02

Asymptotic separation · k ≥ 3

The real-valued ratio tends to zero.

FO(j,k,n) / FD(j,k,n) ⟶ 0

For each fixed natural j and k ≥ 3 as n tends to infinity.

03

Eventual comparison · k ≥ 3

The denominator statistic eventually dominates.

FO(j,k,n) < FD(j,k,n)

There is an explicit formal existence claim for a threshold beyond which the inequality holds.
