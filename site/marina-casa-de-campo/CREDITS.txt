Marina Casa de Campo playable foundation

Built from the prepared ageofresearch/tidewater-rocha working tree, reusing
Tidewater systems by dgreenheck / DRG Software Solutions LLC and Three.js.
See LICENSE.txt and THREE-LICENSE.txt for the accompanying MIT license notices.

Coast, road, quay, pier and building footprints are authored foundation geometry
from registered reference observations. Google imagery is research material;
no Google map images, tiles or live imagery services are included at runtime.
Heights and seabed values are provisional local construction values, not
surveyed levels or navigation depths.

Soccer ball v2: original 22 cm Blender model and authored wear, with CC0 Dry Ground Rocks and Brown Leather photographic material references by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven.
Packed PBR atlas, independent leather detail, modeled soil grains and grass clippings.
See marina/soccer-ball/CREDITS.txt and provenance-v2.json.
