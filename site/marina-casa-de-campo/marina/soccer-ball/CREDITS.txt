Soil diffuse/normal references retrieved from adithya-s-k/RCForge on GitHub. Leather reference retrieved from SethRobinson/Patchy on GitHub. See provenance-v2.json for exact file hashes and retrieval URLs. Original CC0 assets were photographed/scanned; derivative contact masks, chlorophyll transfer and pebble grain are authored for this ball.

The runtime includes only the ball, packed PBR maps and a tiled detail map. No photographic pitch, studio environment, camera or light is exported. The original Blender scene is preserved separately.
