Independent scrutiny

Choose the question you are qualified to answer.

A useful report does not need to review everything. formAGIzation accepts narrow, attributable reviews that state their scope precisely and avoid implying conclusions outside it.

Five review scopes

Different expertise produces different evidence.

01

Reproduction review

Run the documented commands in a clean environment and record the toolchain, platform, outputs, deviations, and result.

02

Kernel and trusted-base review

Inspect compiler and dependency pins, theorem axioms, unsafe features, external checkers, certificates, and the claimed trusted computing base.

03

Semantic-alignment review

Compare the informal target against formal domains, quantifiers, definitions, hypotheses, boundary conventions, and public conclusions.

04

Subject-matter review

Evaluate whether the definitions, argument architecture, conventions, and mathematical interpretation are appropriate for the field.

05

Novelty review

Compare the exact formalized statements and proof ingredients with the literature. State databases searched, date, and uncertainty.

What a review report records

  • artifact identifier, version, and commit or release reviewed;
  • reviewer name or stable identity and relevant expertise;
  • declared conflicts of interest;
  • the exact review scope;
  • methods, environment, and evidence examined;
  • findings, limitations, and unresolved questions;
  • the review date.

Reports are preserved append-only. A later correction or disagreement adds evidence to the record rather than deleting the original report.

How to begin

  1. 01

    Select an artifact and scope

    Start with the evidence dossier and identify the one dimension you intend to assess.

  2. 02

    Work from a fixed revision

    Record the commit, toolchain, dependencies, and any local changes before drawing conclusions.

  3. 03

    Publish the evidence

    Open a GitHub issue or pull request containing the report. Maintainers will preserve an accepted report under the artifact’s review directory.
