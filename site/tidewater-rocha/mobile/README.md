# Mobile yacht zones

Build `yacht-daylight-1`. The mobile route connects boarding, the aft terrace,
swim platform, main saloon, upper terrace, solarium, upper interior, bridge, forward lounge and aft lower guest distributor. All zone
packages, collision indices and GPU profiles finish preparation under the initial
loader. The standalone mobile and full tours use open sky around the yacht.
Open **Luz solar** in the walking menu to set the sun direction relative to the bow
and its height above the horizon. **Restablecer luz** returns to the reference daylight.

The mobile renderer keeps an opaque and a glass batch in one 4× MSAA pass.
Changing the sun angle refreshes one orthographic depth map for the entire yacht.
The 512² map covers the entire yacht, including the antenna. Each sun sector
retains the cast-shadow surfaces that can be visible from that direction.
The largest walking profile submits 24,656 triangles. Camera-independent
material lighting is prepared once per solar angle. Polished metal and glass
continue to respond to the viewing direction. A separate
offline bake traces light from each compartment to its first opaque surface and
then toward the sun. It records 24 incident directions and six broad reflection
lobes per compartment. Regenerate these assets with
`node scripts/yacht-geometry/bake-yacht-daylight.mjs`. Deck contact lighting
is generated by `scripts/yacht-geometry/bake-walk-light.mjs`.

draws, one pass, render scale 1 and MSAA 4.

During generation, each opaque face is checked against the walk camera's
possible positions. A face pointing away from the entire camera volume of a
longitudinal band can be skipped. The remaining faces occupy one contiguous
range in the resident index buffer for that band. This uses the actual triangle
winding and a conservative margin. Glass keeps its original ordering. Moving
the camera selects an index offset in the same mesh. The gangway and upper interior have separate ranges ordered from near to far.
Their prefixes cover all potentially visible geometry when the whole frustum
points down at the gangway or aft from the upper interior. All ranges are
exercised before the route opens. Across the five final profiles approximately
8.7 MiB of geometry stays resident.

`tests/yacht-gangway-fidelity.test.mjs` checks the exact source faces of the
hanging cables, chairs and piping against the actual submitted index ranges.

`tests/yacht-saloon.cjs` checks identical doorway pixels after walking from
both directions. `tests/yacht-saloon-performance.cjs` compares first and
repeated crossings in three fresh contexts per version. Serve an immutable
reference on a separate port and pass `YACHT_REFERENCE_BASE` to the script.

## Gangway teak finish

`gangway-teak-1` fixes material inheritance before compaction and simplification.
Raw boarding parts that supplied only `materialID` previously acquired surface
code zero from the sun-deck package onward. The three panels now retain their
shared material in every fallback and active profile; explicit per-vertex zero
remains valid. Unknown materials fail offline generation.

The [finish comparison](../teak-audit/) records fixed-camera views and checks.
Each unchanged 764 mm inset uses seven 105.714 mm boards with six 4 mm seams.
This is an approved provisional layout, not a surveyed vessel specification.
Shared base colour is #B69A73 sRGB, roughness .80, metalness and clearcoat zero;
board brightness varies by at most ±5%. Dark caulking and longitudinal fibres
are filtered at a distance. Borders and landings receive the same finish, with
cross-grain on end borders and the existing landing pitch. The lake pier and
other deck colours are not recoloured. Geometry, support and collisions are exact.

A fourth 256×256 layer in the existing RG8 material array adds 174,762 bytes
including mipmaps. It stores RG16-encoded combined grain, board variation and caulking,
so the mobile fragment shader remains identical to the prior release. For the
constant-matte boarding codes 37..42, aSurface.xy carries precomputed atlas UVs;
both renderers decode roughness .8 and metalness zero for these codes. This
keeps the four-buffer interface and all resident geometry byte counts unchanged.
The full renderer passes those UVs in a separate smooth varying; its existing
material-code varying stays flat. Both renderers must pass the rendered six-seam
probe, preventing constant UVs from silently removing the finish.
Total mobile finish storage is 3,495,255 bytes, under 3.6 MB;
there are still two sampled texture resources, two draws and one pass. All
buffers, textures and pipelines are prepared before the first walk. The original
three texture layers are checked byte-for-byte against the previous release.
