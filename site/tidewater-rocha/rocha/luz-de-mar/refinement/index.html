Rendering and reference notes

The model uses 12 shared batches, no added textures and no additional lighting or reflection passes. Water quality, resolution and anti-aliasing are unchanged. The paired software-GPU timings are diagnostic; they do not measure Android/browser FPS.

987 registered display parts compile into 62,923 triangles. Bathroom mirrors use an analytic room-box approximation and do not reflect moving objects. Complete stair routes and technical-space finishes remain provisional. The full map requires WebGPU with 32 sampled textures per stage.

References: vessel listing · earlier saloon photograph · earlier owner suite. Manufacturer photographs describe the model series; the uploaded source establishes the provisional layout.
